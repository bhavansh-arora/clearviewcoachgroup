Good to know

Career coaching, answered.

I don't know what I want next. Is coaching still for me?

That's exactly where most people start. Our Career Transition programme is built to take you from unclear to a plan, mapping your skills, your options and the paths you haven't considered yet.

Do you help with CVs and LinkedIn?

Yes. CV, LinkedIn and personal brand are part of many programmes, and Personal Branding (from €295) is a dedicated two-hour session.

Something's gone wrong at work. How fast can we talk?

The Career Crisis Clinic exists for exactly that: one focused 90-minute troubleshoot. Mention the urgency when you enquire and we'll prioritise your first conversation.

Can I buy coaching for my son, daughter or partner?

Yes. Student and graduate programmes start at €195, and you can design a gift voucher for any programme.
