Meet the founder

Jane Downes. Straight talk, serious results.

Jane founded Clearview in 2004 after a career in recruitment and HR consulting. Twenty-two years and more than 23,000 coaching hours later, she is one of Ireland's best-known career performance coaches and a regular voice on all things career in the national media.

Clients describe her as a sounding board and a challenger: someone who sharpens your positioning, stress-tests your thinking and brings structure and candour to the moves most people leave to chance.
