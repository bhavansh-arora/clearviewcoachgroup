The real challenge

Acting the part isn't the same as having a plan.

Leading in today's market means building career resilience while you adapt to globalisation, tighter budgets, stricter reporting deadlines, multicultural teams and constant ambiguity. Through all of it you need to make good decisions and build relationships that are productive and impactful.

The major challenge for experienced executives is showing their individual style and competencies, and demonstrating personal effectiveness at its best. Leaders also need career strategy: mapping their options, protecting their brand and reputation, and staying ready for whatever opportunity comes next. This is where we come in.

Completely. What you bring to a session stays between you and your coach. If your organisation is funding the work, we agree with you up front what, if anything, is shared.

Online or in person?

Both. Sessions run by video or in person in Dublin, and we work with leaders across Ireland and around the world.

One session or a programme?

Some leaders need one sharp session, from €395. Most choose a programme, from €745, so there's time to apply what we work on between sessions. We'll recommend the right shape during your consultation.

Can my company pay for it?

Often, yes. Clearview partners with organisations most days of the working week. Use the funding request generator above to make the case to your manager or HR in minutes.

What happens in the first conversation?

A brief exploratory discussion by phone or video, before any coaching starts. You'll come away knowing whether we're the right fit, who you'd work with and what it would involve.
