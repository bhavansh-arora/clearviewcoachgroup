Our story

You have more room for manoeuvre than you think.

Jane Downes founded Clearview in 2004 after an extensive career in recruitment and HR consulting. Since then, Clearview has partnered with businesses from brand-name multinationals to semi-state organisations, supporting leaders, managers, teams and individuals through growth, performance, transitions, redundancies, engagement and wellbeing.

Our programmes have been built and refined over many years, and we bring only the most powerful tools into personalised one-to-one coaching and group sessions. The aim never changes: help you decide on the impact you want to make, and find a way to start making it.

Our vision

To be the trusted leader in career and performance coaching, inspiring lasting success and impact worldwide.

Our mission

Empowering professionals and organisations to achieve career success and performance growth.
